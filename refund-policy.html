Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains when you can cancel an order and how refunds work for services ordered from Sabiha Sultana.

Cancelling before work starts

If you cancel before I have started work on your order, you will receive a full refund.

Cancelling after work starts

If you cancel after work has started, you may receive a partial refund for the part of the confirmed scope that has not been delivered yet.

If the service was not delivered as agreed

If the service was not delivered as described in the written offer, tell me within 7 days of delivery. I will first offer to fix it; if that is not possible, you will receive a full or partial refund depending on what was delivered.

When refunds are not available

  • After the confirmed service has been fully delivered as agreed.
  • For dissatisfaction with results outside my control, such as replies or sales from your outreach.
  • For costs of third-party products you bought yourself, including any LinkedIn subscription.

How to request a refund

Message me on WhatsApp (+880 1580-539176) or Telegram (@sabihaoishhee) with your name, order details, and the reason for the request.

Processing time

Approved refunds are sent back using the original payment method within 7 business days. The time it takes to show in your account depends on your payment provider.
